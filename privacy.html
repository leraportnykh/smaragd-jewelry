What we collect

When you place an order, book a private viewing or write to us, we collect your name, email, phone number and, for deliveries, your address. For insured delivery of high-value pieces we may also ask for an ID document at handover. When you subscribe to our letters we keep your email.

Your basket and favourites are stored only in your own browser.

How we use it

We use your data to deliver your order, to arrange viewings, to answer your messages and, if you subscribed, to send you a few letters a year about new pieces and rare stones. We never sell your data.

Who we share it with

Only with the services needed to fulfil your order: the insured courier, the insurer and the payment provider. Card details are entered on the provider's secure page and never reach us.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what data we hold about you, correct it or delete it. You can unsubscribe from our letters with one click.

Contact

Questions about your data: salon@smaragd.jewelry.
