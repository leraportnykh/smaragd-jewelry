About us

A Kyiv house of fine jewelry, built around one stone: the emerald.

Smaragd is a small house of jewelers where every piece begins with a stone chosen by eye, drawn by hand, cast in gold and set with care by the same few pairs of hands.

2014

Founded in Kyiv as a workshop of three; today a salon and workshop on Velyka Zhytomyrska Street.

12

Masters under one roof: jewelers, setters, a wax carver and our own gemologist.

1,400+

Emeralds chosen by hand, one at a time. Every stone over one carat comes with an independent GRS or Gübelin report.

Lifetime

Care for every piece: yearly cleaning, setting checks and resizing in the first year, free of charge.

Our story

The stone comes first

We buy emeralds one at a time, mostly from Colombia and Zambia, and we look at colour before we look at weight. A stone may wait in our safe for a year until we know what it should become.

Every diamond we set is natural and conflict-free. Most of our pieces exist only once; the rest are made in numbered editions of no more than five.

Looking for a stone of your own? We take a few bespoke commissions every season, from the search for the emerald to the final setting.
