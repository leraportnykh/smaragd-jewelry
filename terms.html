Orders

An order is accepted once your advisor confirms it by phone or email. Most pieces exist once; if a piece is sold before we confirm, we refund the full amount.

Prices & payment

Prices are shown in US dollars and include taxes for Ukraine. International customs duties, if any, are paid by the customer. Card payments are processed by our payment provider; larger orders can be paid by bank transfer.

Price on request

Pieces from $10,000 are sold only after a private viewing. The price, payment terms and delivery are agreed in writing with your advisor before the sale.

Delivery

Delivery times and costs are listed on the To customers page. Every piece is insured until it reaches you and is handed over against ID.

Returns

Unworn and unaltered pieces with their case and reports can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Bespoke, resized and engraved pieces are final sale.

Natural stones

Emeralds are natural stones with inclusions and, in most cases, traditional oil treatment, as stated in their laboratory reports. Such characteristics are part of the stone and are not considered defects.
