Private viewing

Any piece in our catalog can be shown to you privately: in our salon at 17 Velyka Zhytomyrska Street, Kyiv, at your home or by video call. Pieces marked “Price on request” are sold only this way.

  • Book through the button on any product page or on the contact page.
  • Your advisor confirms the date within one working day.
  • A viewing is free and places you under no obligation.

Delivery

Every piece is fully insured until it is in your hands and travels in a SMARAGD case with its reports.

  • Ukraine: free armoured courier, 1–3 working days, handed over against ID.
  • Worldwide: insured delivery, 5–10 working days, $150. Customs duties are paid by the recipient.
  • Collection at the salon (17 Velyka Zhytomyrska St, Kyiv) by appointment, with a private fitting.

Returns & exchange

You can return or exchange a piece within 14 days of delivery if it has not been worn, has not been resized and comes back with its case and reports. Bespoke and engraved pieces cannot be returned.

Write to us at salon@smaragd.jewelry with your order number and we will arrange an insured pickup.

Certificates

Each piece comes with a SMARAGD certificate of authenticity describing the metal and every stone. Emeralds over one carat carry an independent laboratory report (GRS or Gübelin) on origin and treatment; major diamonds are graded by GIA.

Care

Emeralds are hard but carry natural inclusions, the “jardin”, and are traditionally oiled. Keep them away from ultrasonic cleaners, steam, heat and household chemicals, and take rings off for sport and gardening.

Store each piece separately in its case. Wipe with a soft dry cloth after wearing. Once a year we check the settings and refresh the polish free of charge.

Questions

Why is there no price on some pieces?
Pieces from $10,000 are unique and their price depends on the final choice of size and setting, so we present them in person. Book a private viewing and your advisor will confirm the price.
Are your emeralds treated?
Most emeralds in the world are traditionally oiled to calm their natural inclusions. The laboratory report that comes with each stone states the exact degree of treatment, from none to moderate.
Can you resize a ring?
Yes, within two sizes and free of charge in the first year. Eternity bands and some sculpted rings cannot be resized; your advisor will tell you before you buy.
Can I bring my own stone?
Yes. We take a few bespoke commissions every season, including pieces made around a family stone. Tell us about it on the contact page.
